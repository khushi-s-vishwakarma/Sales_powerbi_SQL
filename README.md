# 🚀 Sales Dashboard Project | Power BI + SQL

## 🔍 The Problem

At Atliq company, the sales team was struggling to make sense of their growing sales data. Reports were scattered, insights were hard to extract, and each month analysts spent hours manually preparing Excel files just to understand basic performance metrics.

❓Key questions like:
- Who are our top-performing customers?
- Which regions are underperforming?
- Where are we making the most profit?
- How are sales trending month over month?

…remained unanswered or took too long to answer.

---

## 💡 The Solution: A Sales Dashboard

To solve this, I built an **interactive Power BI dashboard** using sales data (~100,000 rows), which allows the sales team to instantly see:
- Monthly revenue trends
- Top 5 customers by revenue
- Market-wise performance
- Profit insights and contribution by region

The goal was to make insights **easily accessible**, **automated**, and **actionable** — so decision-makers could spend more time making decisions and less time gathering data.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

- **Power BI** – For dashboard design and interactivity
- **SQL** – For data cleaning, aggregation, and transformation
- **Excel** – As the original data source with over 1L rows
- **DAX** – For creating calculated columns and measures

---

## 🧠 Key Learnings

- How to **translate a business problem into a dashboard**
- Data modeling and relationships in Power BI
- Writing meaningful **DAX measures** and using slicers/filters for better usability
- Preparing queries in SQL to make the data ready for visualization
